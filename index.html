About Me

Designing with purpose,
building with precision.

I started in education, analysing over 5,000 student answer copies as an academic evaluator. That work trained me to spot patterns in qualitative data at scale — the same skill I now use to synthesize user interviews and usability test findings into actionable design decisions.

Now I apply that analytical mindset to product design. As Founder & Product Designer of HiLi Notes, I've conducted 6+ user interviews, defined flows and interfaces, and collaborated with AI tools to ship a live PWA with 20+ beta users and strong retention. With TimeWise, I explored the full UX process—from research and personas to hi‑fi prototypes and usability testing.

My process:

  • Start with users — I conduct interviews and research to validate real problems
  • Design clearly — I create user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity interfaces in Figma
  • Build with AI — I use AI tools to rapidly explore concepts, then apply design judgment to refine, validate, and ship
  • Iterate fast — I use feedback from real users to refine the product

I've recently completed the Google UX Design Professional Certificate and I am looking for an entry‑level UX/UI role where I can keep doing end‑to‑end UX on real products with experienced teams.
